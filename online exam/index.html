Online Examination System

The Online Examination System is a simple web-based application designed to conduct multiple-choice examinations. This exam contains 10 questions based on Internet Programming concepts such as HTML and CSS. Students can select their answers and submit the examination after completing all the questions.

Exam Guidelines

  • Read each question carefully.
  • The exam contains 10 multiple-choice questions.
  • Choose only one answer for each question.
  • Do not refresh the page during the exam.
  • Click the Submit Exam button after completing all questions.
